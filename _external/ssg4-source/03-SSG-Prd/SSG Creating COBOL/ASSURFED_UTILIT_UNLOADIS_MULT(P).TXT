       PROCEDURE DIVISION.    
       DEB. 
           OPEN OUTPUT  BDE.  
           OPEN  INPUT   DISQ.
           MOVE SPACES TO ZBUF.     
           MOVE ALL ' ' TO CLEF.    
       DEBF.
           READ DISQ INTO ZBUF AT END GO TO FBDE.     
           ADD  1  TO  CTR.   
           WRITE  ZBDE  FROM ZBUF.  
           MOVE SPACES TO ZBUF.     
           MOVE  CTR  TO  CTR-1.    
           GO TO DEBF.  
       CLOS.
           CLOSE  BDE.  
           CLOSE  DISQ. 
           STOP  RUN.   
       FBDE.
           DISPLAY  'FIN DE FICHIER' UPON  CONSOLE.   
           MOVE   CTR   TO CTR-1.   
           DISPLAY  MES1   UPON  CONSOLE. 
           GO TO  CLOS.
