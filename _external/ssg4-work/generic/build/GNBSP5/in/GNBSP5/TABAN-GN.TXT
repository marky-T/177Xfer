       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
      * User story: 121355
      * SSG-4: one TABAN for every call. Amount fields, key
      * parts, NEURO list and the sequence check come from the SGSPARM
      * file written by the skeleton hand-off; behaviour:
      * Generic/TABAN/spec.md. The RDMS session of the skeleton is not
      * kept (spec A2); EUU003 is called as in the skeleton (spec A3).
       PROGRAM-ID. 'TABAN-GN'.
       ENVIRONMENT DIVISION.
       CONFIGURATION SECTION.
       SOURCE-COMPUTER. UNIVAC-1100-60EIS.
       OBJECT-COMPUTER. UNIVAC-1100-60EIS.
       SPECIAL-NAMES.
           DECIMAL-POINT IS COMMA.
       INPUT-OUTPUT SECTION.
       FILE-CONTROL.
           SELECT PARMF ASSIGN TO DISC SGSPARM.
           SELECT FI1 ASSIGN TO DISC TABIN.
           SELECT IMP ASSIGN TO PRINTER.

       77  P-SEQ              PIC 9 VALUE 0.
       77  CP-LEN             PIC 9(5) COMP VALUE 0.
       77  KP                 PIC 9(5) COMP VALUE 0.
       77  KO                 PIC 9(5) COMP VALUE 0.

       77  N-IN               PIC 9(3) COMP VALUE 0.
       77  I                  PIC 9(3) COMP VALUE 0.

       77  S                  PIC 9(3) COMP VALUE 0.

       77  NB-LUS             PIC 9(10) COMP VALUE 0.

       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       DEBUT.
           MOVE ALL 'N' TO NE-TAB.
           OPEN INPUT PARMF.
       PARM-LECT.
           READ PARMF AT END GO TO PARM-FIN.
           IF P-TYPE = 'HD'
              MOVE P-POS TO P-INLEN
              MOVE P-LEN TO P-SEQ.
           IF P-TYPE = 'KY'
              ADD 1 TO N-KY
              MOVE P-POS TO KY-POS (N-KY)
              MOVE P-LEN TO KY-LEN (N-KY).
           IF P-TYPE = 'IN'
              ADD 1 TO N-IN
              MOVE P-POS TO IN-POS (N-IN)
              MOVE P-LEN TO IN-LEN (N-IN).
           IF P-TYPE = 'NE'
              MOVE 'Y' TO NE-FLAG (P-POS).
           GO TO PARM-LECT.
       PARM-FIN.
           CLOSE PARMF.
           MOVE ZEROES TO AMOUNTS.
           OPEN INPUT FI1.
           OPEN OUTPUT IMP.
           MOVE 'TABAN'     TO NM-PGM-APL   OF WS-CMT-PGM.
           MOVE 'BATCH'     TO NM-NOM-ENV   OF WS-CMT-PGM.
           MOVE 'Y'         TO CD-DBG-PRG   OF WS-CMT-PGM.
           MOVE 'EUW002'    TO NM-NOM-COP   OF WS-CMT-PGM.
           MOVE 'SERVICE1'  TO NM-NOM-DEM   OF WS-CMT-PGM.
           MOVE SPACES      TO WS-PTR-ENE   OF EUW002.
           MOVE 'R'         TO FL-PLT-SGN   OF EUW002.
           MOVE '2'         TO FL-TRD-SGN   OF EUW002.
           MOVE 'Y'         TO MT-ENE-SGN   OF EUW002.
           MOVE '1'         TO CD-FMT-MON-SRT  OF EUW002.
           MOVE 'Y'         TO FL-IMP-PNT      OF EUW002.
           MOVE 'N'         TO FL-000          OF EUW002.
       LECT.
           MOVE SPACES TO RECIN.
           READ FI1 INTO RECIN AT END GO TO FINBDE.
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-IN.
           MOVE WS-IN-LEN TO CP-LEN.
           IF CP-LEN > P-INLEN
              MOVE P-INLEN TO CP-LEN.
           IF CP-LEN > 0
              MOVE RECIN (1:CP-LEN) TO WS-IN (1:CP-LEN).
      * amounts: not numeric -> every space becomes 0 in the record
      * (skeleton: MOVE ZEROES when all spaces, else PMONT<i>)
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       AMT-LOOP.
           IF I > N-KY GO TO AMT-END.
           PERFORM AMT-LOAD.
           IF WS-AMT NOT NUMERIC
              INSPECT WS-IN (KY-POS (I):KY-LEN (I))
                REPLACING ALL SPACE BY '0'
              PERFORM AMT-LOAD.
           MOVE WS-AMT TO REC-AMT (I).
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO AMT-LOOP.
       AMT-END.
           ADD 1 TO NB-LUS.
      * key = the INDIC parts in card order
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-KEY.
           MOVE 1 TO KP.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       KEY-LOOP.
           IF I > N-IN GO TO KEY-END.
           MOVE WS-IN (IN-POS (I):IN-LEN (I))
             TO WS-KEY (KP:IN-LEN (I)).
           ADD IN-LEN (I) TO KP.
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO KEY-LOOP.
       KEY-END.
           COMPUTE KEY-LEN = KP - 1.
           IF FIRS = 0
              MOVE 1 TO FIRS
              GO TO CONT1.
           IF N-IN = 0 GO TO LECT2.
           IF P-SEQ = 1
              IF PREV-KEY (1:KEY-LEN) > WS-KEY (1:KEY-LEN)
                 GO TO ERR-SEQ.
           IF PREV-KEY (1:KEY-LEN) NOT = WS-KEY (1:KEY-LEN)
              GO TO RUPT.
       LECT2.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       LECT2-LOOP.
           IF I > N-KY GO TO LECT.
           ADD REC-AMT (I) TO SUB-AMT (I).
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO LECT2-LOOP.
       RUPT.
           PERFORM DO-RUPT THRU DO-RUPT-EXIT.
       CONT1.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       CONT1-LOOP.
           IF I > N-KY GO TO CONT1-END.
           MOVE ZEROES TO SUB-AMT (I).
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO CONT1-LOOP.
       CONT1-END.
           MOVE WS-KEY TO PREV-KEY.
           GO TO LECT2.
       FINBDE.
           IF N-IN > 0
              PERFORM DO-RUPT THRU DO-RUPT-EXIT
              GO TO FIN-TOTAL.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       FINBDE-LOOP.
           IF I > N-KY GO TO FIN-TOTAL.
           ADD SUB-AMT (I) TO TOT-AMT (I).
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO FINBDE-LOOP.
       FIN-TOTAL.
           MOVE '*****TOTAL GENERAL *****'  TO  LIGNE.
           WRITE  LIGNE  AFTER PAGE.
           MOVE 'T' TO WS-WHICH.
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-FA-VAL.
           PERFORM AMT-LINES THRU AMT-LINES-EXIT.
           MOVE  NB-LUS  TO   P-LUS.
           WRITE  LIGNE  FROM  TIT-TOTAL  AFTER  3.
       FF. CLOSE  FI1.
           CLOSE  IMP.
           STOP RUN.
       ERR-SEQ.
           MOVE      '**ERREUR DE SEQUENCE**'  TO  LIGNE.
           WRITE  LIGNE  AFTER  5.
           MOVE '*ANCIEN INDIC*' TO WS-RUPT.
           MOVE PREV-KEY TO WS-PRT-KEY.
           PERFORM IND-LINE THRU IND-LINE-EXIT.
           MOVE '*NOUVEL INDIC*' TO WS-RUPT.
           MOVE WS-KEY TO WS-PRT-KEY.
           PERFORM IND-LINE THRU IND-LINE-EXIT.
           GO TO FF.
      * a group ends: break line of the previous key, its sub-totals,
      * sub-totals added to the grand totals (skeleton RUPT)
       DO-RUPT.
           MOVE '***RUPTURE*** ' TO WS-RUPT.
           MOVE PREV-KEY TO WS-PRT-KEY.
           PERFORM IND-LINE THRU IND-LINE-EXIT.
           MOVE 'S' TO WS-WHICH.
           MOVE '*SOUS-TOTAL*' TO WS-FA-VAL.
           PERFORM AMT-LINES THRU AMT-LINES-EXIT.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       DO-RUPT-LOOP.
           IF I > N-KY GO TO DO-RUPT-EXIT.
           ADD SUB-AMT (I) TO TOT-AMT (I).
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO DO-RUPT-LOOP.
       DO-RUPT-EXIT.
           EXIT.
      * the break line: WS-RUPT (1:14) + every key part of WS-PRT-KEY
      * followed by one space (skeleton TIT-RUPT1, AFTER 2)
       IND-LINE.
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-RUPT (15:4086).
           MOVE 15 TO KP.
           MOVE 1 TO KO.
           MOVE 1 TO I.
       IND-LOOP.
           IF I > N-IN GO TO IND-WRITE.
           MOVE WS-PRT-KEY (KO:IN-LEN (I)) TO WS-RUPT (KP:IN-LEN (I)).
           ADD IN-LEN (I) TO KO.
           ADD IN-LEN (I) TO KP.
           ADD 1 TO KP.
           ADD 1 TO I.
           GO TO IND-LOOP.
       IND-WRITE.
           WRITE LIGNE FROM WS-RUPT AFTER 2.
       IND-LINE-EXIT.
           EXIT.
      * the amount lines, 4 amounts a line (skeleton IMP-SUB-TOTAL /
      * IMP-TOTAL): NEURO -> Z(18), else EUU003; WS-WHICH S or T
       AMT-LINES.
           MOVE 17     TO MT-ENE-CHI OF EUW002.
           MOVE 25     TO LG-MAX-MON-SRT OF EUW002.
           MOVE 1 TO G.
       AMT-L-GROUP.
           IF G > N-KY GO TO AMT-LINES-EXIT.
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-LIG.
           MOVE WS-FA-VAL TO WS-FA.
           MOVE 1 TO S.
       AMT-L-SLOT.
           IF S > 4 GO TO AMT-L-WRITE.
           COMPUTE K = G + S - 1.
           IF K > N-KY GO TO AMT-L-WRITE.
           IF WS-WHICH = 'S'
              MOVE SUB-AMT (K) TO WS-VAL
           ELSE
              MOVE TOT-AMT (K) TO WS-VAL.
           IF NE-FLAG (K) = 'Y'
              MOVE WS-VAL TO ZONE-25
              MOVE ZONE-25 TO WS-MONT (S)
           ELSE
              MOVE WS-VAL TO MT-ENE OF EUW002
              MOVE EUW002 TO VL-CHP-TSF OF WS-CMT-PGM
              CALL 'EUU003' USING WS-CMT-PGM
              MOVE VL-CHP-TSF OF WS-CMT-PGM TO EUW002
              MOVE MT-SRT OF EUW002 TO WS-MONT (S).
           ADD 1 TO S.
           GO TO AMT-L-SLOT.
       AMT-L-WRITE.
           WRITE LIGNE FROM WS-LIG AFTER 1.
           ADD 4 TO G.
           GO TO AMT-L-GROUP.
       AMT-LINES-EXIT.
           EXIT.
      * amount I of WS-IN, right-aligned with leading zeros in WS-AMT
       AMT-LOAD.
           MOVE ALL '0' TO WS-AMT-X.
           COMPUTE AP = 19 - KY-LEN (I).
           MOVE WS-IN (KY-POS (I):KY-LEN (I))
             TO WS-AMT-X (AP:KY-LEN (I)).
