       PROCEDURE DIVISION.    
       DEBUT.     
           OPEN INPUT  BDE1.  *> SSG-STUB [*I]
           OPEN  OUTPUT  DISQ.
       LECT.
           GO TO LECT1 FINB  DEPENDING  ON FF.  
       LECT1.     
           MOVE SPACES TO Z1.  *> SSG-STUB [*I]
           READ  BDE1  INTO  WREC  AT END ADD 1 TO FF  GO TO LECT.
           GO TO CONT.  
       CONT.
           MOVE KEYA     TO  CLEF.  
           MOVE KEYB     TO  CLEG.  
           MOVE KEYA     TO  CLEF.  
           MOVE KEYB     TO  CLEG.  
           MOVE KEYC     TO  CLEH.  
           ADD  1  TO  CTRIN. 
           WRITE ZDISQ FROM WREC INVALID KEY GO TO  ERRDISQ.
           MOVE SPACES TO WREC WREC1 ZDISQ.     
           GO TO LECT.  
       FINB.
           DISPLAY 'NB.RECORDS ECRITS'  CTRIN  UPON PRINTER.
           CLOSE  DISQ. 
           CLOSE  BDE1.  *> SSG-STUB [*I]
           STOP RUN.    
       ERRDISQ.   
           DISPLAY 'IS.ERROR'  CLEF  UPON  PRINTER.   
           ENTER ASM 'ABORT'.
